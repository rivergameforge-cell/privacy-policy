1. 수집하는 정보

Royal Slime Pop은 회원가입 없이 익명으로 동작합니다. 이름, 이메일, 전화번호, 정확한 위치, 연락처, 사진은 수집하지 않습니다. 수집 또는 처리하는 정보는 다음으로 한정됩니다.

  • 익명 기기 식별자: 온라인 랭킹 참여를 위한 익명 계정 인증에 사용됩니다.
  • 닉네임: 이용자가 직접 입력한 경우에 한해 리더보드 표시용으로 저장됩니다.
  • 게임 기록: 최고 점수 등 랭킹에 필요한 게임 데이터입니다.
  • 광고 식별자 및 광고 상호작용 데이터: Google AdMob이 광고 제공과 측정을 위해 처리합니다.
  • 기술 정보: 광고 및 백엔드 SDK가 처리하는 기기 모델, OS 버전, 대략적 지역 등 표준 정보입니다.

기기 내부에는 진행 상황, 설정, 보유 재화(Slime Drops) 등이 로컬로 저장됩니다.

2. 이용 목적

  • 온라인 랭킹 제공 및 점수 제출/조회
  • 광고 게재와 부정 클릭 방지 및 광고 성과 측정
  • 게임 저장/복원, 오류 진단 및 서비스 개선

1. Information We Collect

Royal Slime Pop works anonymously without any account. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos. We collect or process only:

  • Anonymous device identifier: used to authenticate an anonymous account for the online leaderboard.
  • Nickname: stored for leaderboard display only if you choose to enter one.
  • Game data: high scores and other ranking data.
  • Advertising identifier and ad-interaction data: processed by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads.
  • Technical information: standard data such as device model, OS version, and approximate region processed by the ad/backend SDKs.

Your progress, settings, and in-game currency (Slime Drops) are stored locally on your device.

2. How We Use It

  • Provide the online leaderboard, including score submission and retrieval
  • Serve ads and prevent invalid ad activity
  • Save/restore your game, diagnose errors, and improve the service

3. Third-Party Services

This app uses the following third-party SDKs, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob (advertising): https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Microsoft PlayFab (backend / leaderboard): https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. Information is shared only as needed to provide the services above, or when required by law.

5. Retention & Deletion

  • Local data is removed when you uninstall the app.
  • Server (PlayFab) data such as anonymous ID, nickname, and score can be deleted by request. Email rivergameforge@gmail.com and we will delete it after verification.
  • Ad privacy settings can be reopened from Settings > Ad Privacy Settings in the app to manage Google AdMob consent and ad privacy options. Availability may vary by region and consent status.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is not primarily directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child's data was collected, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Security

Data in transit uses standard encryption (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; changes will be reflected here along with the effective date.
