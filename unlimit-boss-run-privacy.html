Google AdMob may process advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and technical device or network information to serve and measure ads, apply frequency controls, and prevent abuse.

2. Purposes

  • Save and restore game progress
  • Submit and display online leaderboard scores

Local data can be removed through the in-game reset function or by uninstalling the app. To request deletion of leaderboard data, email us with the nickname concerned. We will process verified requests within a reasonable period.

The game is not primarily directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
